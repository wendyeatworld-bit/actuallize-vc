The internet made doctors more expensive. AI is about to make them cheap.

Actualize is a seed fund. We back founders who use AI to make expert care — medical, financial, legal, technical — fast, affordable and actually available to the people who need it.

The thesis

Every expert industry is a doctor's office with three layers.

The internet moved information around. It rebuilt everything made of information and left everything that needed a licensed human untouched. Doctors, lawyers and accountants got more expensive while everything online got cheaper. AI changes which layer is up for grabs.

Front desk

Finding, booking, paying. The internet fixed this. The next fight is over which assistant the patient trusts.

Doctor's office

A trained person reads your situation and decides. AI makes this cheap, so one expert can do the work of several. This is where we invest.

Operating table

The physical work. Neither technology touches it, which makes the savings in the office layer worth more.

Who wins

Whoever owns the thing that can't be copied.

Last time it was the catalog, the brand and the audience. This time it is the license, the liability, the relationship, and the memory of a person over years. When the reading and the paperwork become cheap, the only question left is who is allowed to act on the result.

Healthcare is the biggest prize: $4.5 trillion of US spend, about $1 trillion of it on administration. But the same shift is reaching finance, law, insurance, education and IT services — roughly $2 trillion more.

What we look for

Three shapes of company.

  1. The agent layerThe back office for a whole industry. Picks and shovels for everyone doing the licensed work.
  2. The licensed rollupBuy the signature, put software under it, and let one expert serve many more people.
  3. One licensed human plus softwareDoctor-level care at nurse-level cost, delivered straight to the consumer.
